Add `MonthPicker` for month-only `YYYY-MM` values such as billing periods. It shares the DatePicker field and popover, shows a localized 3×4 month grid with year navigation, `min`/`max` bounds, current-month and clear actions, and grid keyboard navigation. Use it with `FormField` or the new `field.MonthField` bound field.
