The default theme now uses a common neutral palette: slate greys, a white surface and a blue primary (`brand-700` `#2563eb`), with Tailwind-standard status and chart colours. Projects keep their own branding through tokens; `forestTheme` from `@itclabs/react-ui/theme/presets` reproduces the previous green default, including its neutrals and charts.
